THESIS: The site is a sheet of numbered figure plates drawn by code from real or labeled data; it refuses the hero-plus-card-grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white plotting paper #F7F8FA, blue-black ink #151A2E, one magma-family heat ramp as the only color, hairline plot frames, axis ticks, numbered captions. Archivo expanded and heavy for name and figure numbers, STIX Two Text for captions and prose, tabular figures.

STORY: The visitor learns who Evan is and that Evan is available, sees one live figure prove craft, then reads small multiples whose captions each end in one action.
